What is 8Assist?+

8Assist is a case management workspace for housing authorities and RAAs. It brings client files, documents, tasks, and program workflows together in one place.

Will it work with our existing client data or systems?+

We’ll review your current systems and data with your team, then plan the right migration and setup for your housing program.

Is the information really secure?+

Security is an essential part of handling client information. Request a demo to discuss access controls, data handling, and your team’s specific requirements.

Can we customize our workflows?+

We’ll work with your team to map your process and tailor your workspace to the way your program operates.

When will we see an impact?+

Your first month gives your team time to explore the platform, get comfortable, and see how a shared workspace supports your daily work.
